# Level 1 private cinema

These Blender scenes use metres. The walkthrough assembles them in the Level 1 cinema’s 8 × 12 m footprint. The projector faces the north wall. The rear row stands on a 200 mm slate platform reached by two 100 mm steps.

| Editable scene | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| cinema-architecture.blend | Staggered walnut ceiling boards, formed-concrete wall, individually broken-faced basalt blocks, front-wall battens, sconces, grilles and recessed lights |
| cinema-recliner.blend | Upholstered seat, lumbar cushion, cup holders, welt and woven colour and normal maps |
| cinema-garden-wall.blend | Glass-block lightwell, planted walnut sill and linen-shaded lamp |
| cinema-av.blend | Ceiling projector and two-way floorstanding speaker |
| cinema-shelf-objects.blend | Miniature film projector, brass statuette and potted vine for the cabinet display niche |

The rear glazing occupies the bay beside the existing shop door. The three film cabinets form one run on the west wall, with independently stocked sections for films and anime films. Their rack dimensions respond to the catalogue. Cabinet construction, floor, risers, lighting controls and projector playback are assembled in the web scene.

The exported GLB models and packed textures are served with the site. Repeated seats and speakers share mesh geometry. The separate [packaging library](../packaging/README.md) documents the retail models and their real-world dimensions.
